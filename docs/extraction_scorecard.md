# Extraction scorecard

Generated by `python -m scripts.evaluate_extraction --full --write`. Nothing in this file is typed by hand.

- Reader: rules only (no LLM). OCR engine: Tesseract 5.3.4. Scans are read twice (greyscale and black and white).
- Test documents: layouts generated at random (style, labels, column names, date format, items, amounts), not the bundled samples. Scans are rasterised at 220 dpi, tilted, blurred and given noise.
- Each invoice is a correct invoice for a purchase order that is added to the register, and it goes through the whole pipeline on fresh books. The right outcome is therefore always APPROVE. The auto-approval ceiling is lifted for this run, because the scorecard measures reading, not approval policy.
- **Seen while building**: the first 60 text layouts and the first 24 scanned layouts were looked at during development and drove fixes. **Held out**: the rest were first run after the reader was finished. The held-out rows are the fair measure.
- **Straight through**: read correctly and approved. **False alarm**: read correctly but held. **Caught**: something was misread and the invoice was held for a person. **Silent**: something was misread and the invoice was approved. Silent is the outcome that costs money.

## Silent misreads

None in this run. That is a result for these documents, not a guarantee. These are synthetic scans; real scans vary more. A value that no arithmetic, master-data or PO check can confirm (an invoice number, a date) is trusted only when both reads of the scan agree on it, and a disagreement holds the invoice.
